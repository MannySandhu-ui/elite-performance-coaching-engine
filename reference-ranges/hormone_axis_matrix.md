# Hormone Axis Compounding Relationship Matrix

Multi-marker compounding rules. Never evaluate hormone markers in isolation.

---

## Pattern 1: Low Free T / High SHBG / Normal Total T
**Markers:** Free T LOW + SHBG HIGH + Total T normal
**Interpretation:** SHBG sequestering bioavailable testosterone. Total T is misleading.
**Drivers:** Hypothyroidism raises SHBG. High E2 raises SHBG. Caloric deficit. Alcohol.
**Protocol:** Address SHBG driver first. Optimize insulin sensitivity. Assess Zinc/Boron.

---

## Pattern 2: HPTA Suppression (Exogenous Androgen Use)
**Markers:** LH <1.5 + FSH <1.5 + Total T elevated or normal
**Interpretation:** Exogenous androgen suppressing hypothalamic GnRH -> LH/FSH undetectable.
**Clinical Significance:** Prolonged suppression risks testicular atrophy, fertility impairment.
**Action:** Flag to coach. Refer to supervising physician. Monitor LH/FSH, Prolactin, E2 each check-in.

---

## Pattern 3: Elevated E2 (Aromatase Hyperactivity)
**Markers:** E2 >40 pg/mL males + Total T normal or high
**Interpretation:** Elevated aromatase converting testosterone to estradiol.
**Drivers:** High adipose tissue. Exogenous androgens. Zinc deficiency. Alcohol.
**Protocol:** Identify aromatase driver. Consider DIM, Zinc before AI use.

---

## Pattern 4: Low T / Low LH / Low FSH (Hypogonadotropic)
**Markers:** Total T <400 + LH <2.0 + FSH <2.0 (no exogenous androgens)
**Interpretation:** Central HPTA suppression.
**Primary Drivers:** Caloric deficit <30 kcal/kg LBM/day. Excessive volume. High Prolactin. Sleep deprivation.
**Action:** Rule out energy availability deficit first. Endocrinology referral if persists.

---

## Pattern 5: Low T / High LH / High FSH (Primary Hypogonadism)
**Markers:** Total T <400 + LH >8.0 + FSH >8.0
**Interpretation:** Testicular failure. Pituitary signaling correctly but gonads not responding.
**Action:** Medical referral mandatory. Outside coaching scope.

## Pattern 6: High Cortisol / Low T (HPA-HPG Competition)
**Markers:** Morning Cortisol >22 + Total T <500
**Interpretation:** Chronic HPA axis suppressing HPG axis. Cortisol and GnRH share precursors.
**Drivers:** Overtraining. Sleep deficit. Caloric restriction. Psychological stress.
**Protocol:** Reduce training volume FIRST. Stress reduction is primary intervention.

---

## Pattern 7: Low Free T3 / Normal TSH (Euthyroid Sick)
**Markers:** Free T3 <3.0 + TSH 0.5-2.5 + Free T4 normal
**Interpretation:** Impaired peripheral T4->T3 deiodinase conversion. TSH will not flag this.
**Drivers:** Prolonged caloric deficit. Very low carbs (<100g/day). Selenium deficiency. Elevated cortisol.
**Impact:** Reduces metabolic rate, impairs protein synthesis, elevates fatigue.
**Protocol:** Increase calories/carb cycling. Assess Selenium.

---

## Pattern 8: Elevated Prolactin / Low Libido / Low T
**Markers:** Prolactin >18 + Libido <4/10 + declining Total T
**Interpretation:** Prolactin inhibits GnRH pulsatility -> secondary testosterone suppression.
**Drivers:** Dopaminergic suppression. Opioids. Antipsychotics. Pituitary microadenoma.
**Action:** >25 ng/mL -> endocrinology referral to rule out pituitary pathology.
