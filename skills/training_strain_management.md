# Skill: Vector 4 — Training Strain Management

## Purpose
Correlate biofeedback, recovery markers, and training load data to identify overtraining risk, CNS/PNS imbalance, and systemic stress accumulation. Provide explicit training variable modification recommendations.

---

## Analytical Logic

### Step 1: Recovery Status Assessment
Calculate Recovery Score composite:
- HRV: if >10% below personal baseline -> RED FLAG (high systemic stress)
- Resting HR: if >5 bpm above baseline -> YELLOW FLAG
- Sleep quality <6/10 + duration <7 hrs -> HIGH RECOVERY DEBT
- Subjective energy <5/10 for 2+ consecutive check-ins -> CHRONICALLY UNDER-RECOVERED

### Step 2: Biomarker-Training Correlation
Map key markers to training load context:
- Morning Cortisol >22 + high training volume = HPA axis overload -> volume reduction mandatory
- Free T3 <3.0 + high volume + low calories = adaptive thermogenesis -> calorie and volume adjustment
- CK/AST both elevated (if CK available) -> excessive muscle damage -> recovery modality emphasis
- Ferritin <40 + endurance volume -> impaired oxygen delivery -> iron repletion + volume review
- Low subjective libido (<5/10) + low Total T = systemic androgenic suppression -> recovery phase

### Step 3: Deload Trigger Assessment
MANDATORY DELOAD triggers (recommend immediate):
- Weeks since deload >8 weeks
- HRV >10% below baseline for >2 consecutive weeks
- Two or more: sleep <6/10, energy <4/10, libido <5/10, mood <5/10
- Any emergency biomarker threshold breached
- Subjective joint/tendon pain rated >6/10

RECOMMENDED DELOAD (coach discretion):
- Weeks since deload 6-8 weeks + any two performance decline indicators
- Cortisol >20 sustained + low subjective energy

### Step 4: Volume and Intensity Recommendations
Based on recovery status:
- GREEN (HRV normal, sleep good, subjective >7/10): Maintain or progress 5-10% per week
- YELLOW (HRV -5-10%, sleep fair, subjective 5-7/10): Maintain volume, reduce intensity 10-15%
- RED (HRV >10% below, sleep poor, subjective <5/10): Reduce volume 20-30%, reduce intensity 15-20%
- DELOAD: Reduce volume 40-50%, maintain movement quality, emphasize parasympathetic recovery

### Step 5: Recovery Modality Recommendations
Based on CNS vs. PNS indicator pattern:
- CNS-dominant fatigue (high cortisol, low HRV, high subjective stress): Breathing work, meditation, Zone 1 cardio only, cold contrast therapy
- PNS-dominant (low resting HR adaptation plateau, flat HRV, mechanical fatigue): Sauna, massage, sleep optimization, active rest
