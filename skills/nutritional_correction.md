# Skill: Vector 2 — Nutritional Course Correction

## Purpose
Cross-reference current caloric/macro intake against client anthropometrics, training load, lab data, and fatigue trends. Generate evidence-grounded nutritional adjustments.

---

## Analytical Logic

### Step 1: Energy Availability Assessment
Calculate Energy Availability (EA):
EA = (Daily Calories - Exercise Energy Expenditure) / Lean Body Mass (kg)
- EA <30 kcal/kg LBM: HIGH RISK - HPTA suppression, Free T3 suppression, cortisol elevation, bone density loss
- EA 30-45 kcal/kg LBM: MARGINAL - monitor hormones, thyroid, performance
- EA >45 kcal/kg LBM: ADEQUATE for most athletic goals

### Step 2: Protein Adequacy
For hypertrophy/strength athletes:
- Optimal: 2.0-2.4 g/kg body weight/day
- Minimum: 1.6 g/kg body weight/day
- Check against body weight field in client profile

Flag if protein <1.6 g/kg: Sub-optimal for myofibrillar protein synthesis

### Step 3: Carbohydrate Context
Cross-reference training volume and Free T3:
- If Free T3 <3.0 + carbs <100g/day: flag carbohydrate restriction as likely T3 suppression driver
- High-volume athletes (>15 sets/session): minimum 3-5 g/kg carbohydrates
- Carb timing: recommend 40-60% of carbs periworkout (pre + intra + post)

### Step 4: Biomarker-Driven Nutritional Flags
- Hs-CRP >1.0: Anti-inflammatory dietary emphasis (Omega-3s, colorful vegetables, reduce refined carbs)
- Ferritin <40: Red meat 2-3x/week + Vitamin C with meals. Avoid iron + calcium co-administration.
- Vitamin D <40: Supplement + dietary fat with meals to optimize absorption
- TG >100 + TG:HDL >2.0: Reduce refined carbohydrates and fructose. Increase fiber. Consider Omega-3s.
- Fasting Insulin >8: Carbohydrate redistribution (lower at breakfast, higher periworkout)
- Uric Acid >6.0: Reduce fructose, alcohol, purine-dense foods

### Step 5: Meal Timing Recommendations
Based on training data:
- Pre-workout (1-2 hrs): 0.3-0.4 g/kg protein + 0.5-1.0 g/kg carbs
- Intra-workout (if >90 min): 20-40g easily digestible carbs
- Post-workout (within 2 hrs): 0.4 g/kg protein + 0.8-1.2 g/kg carbs
- Pre-sleep: 30-40g casein or Greek yogurt (leucine threshold met)
